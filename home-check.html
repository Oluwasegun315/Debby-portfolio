About

Debby Tella

Years of Experience

I am a graphic designer with 3+ years of practice, with a strong focus on product label design and packaging visuals. My work helps products feel clear, appealing and ready for the shelf.

The approach is simple: listen closely, design with intention, refine carefully, and stay client-focused without losing the details that make the work feel polished.

Process

01

Discover

We start with your idea, audience and constraints. I ask questions until the brief is clear.

02

Strategy

Then I define the visual direction — tone, references, and the problem the design needs to solve.

03

Design

Concepts are built with intention: type, color, composition and hierarchy working together.

04

Refine

Feedback is folded in carefully so the work gets sharper, not noisier.

05

Deliver

You receive organized files, ready for print, digital or both — with clear usage notes.
